# Niche Templates — Section Blueprints

Section order is a conversion argument. Each section answers the visitor's next question.
Read the relevant niche section before building.

---

## CLINIC / DENTAL / MEDICAL

**Visitor mindset:** Anxious, needs trust before action. Wants to know: Is this place legit? Will it hurt? Can I afford it?

**Section order:**
1. **Hero** — Treatment name + outcome claim + trust signal ("1,200+ מטופלים מרוצים") + CTA ("קביעת תור")
2. **Pain Acknowledgment** — Empathetic opener: "אנחנו יודעים שביקור אצל רופא השיניים יכול לעורר חרדה."
3. **Why Us** — 3–4 differentiators with icons: ✓ ניסיון 15 שנה · ✓ הרדמה ממוחשבת · ✓ תשלומים גמישים
4. **Services** — Cards with treatment name, benefit, and estimated duration. No price (leads to call).
5. **How It Works** — 3-step process (תור → טיפול → מעקב) with numbers and brief text
6. **Social Proof** — Real testimonials (name, treatment type, star rating — never fake), or remove entirely
7. **Team** — Real photos, real credentials. Placeholder: `[תמונת ד״ר [שם]]`, `[מס׳ רישיון]`
8. **Kupat Cholim** — Which funds recognized: כללית / מכבי / מאוחדת / לאומית with logos
9. **FAQ** — 6–8 questions. Real objections: "האם כואב?" "כמה זה עולה?" "כמה זמן לוקח?"
10. **CTA Section** — WhatsApp + phone + booking form (with honeypot)

**Aesthetic:** Clean light mode (elderly-friendly). Frank Ruhl Libre or Assistant for Hebrew. Calm blue/teal or sage/white palette. Never dark mode.

## RESTAURANT / FOOD / CAFE

**Visitor mindset:** Hungry, browsing on phone, wants to visualize the experience.

**Section order:**
1. **Hero** — Atmospheric food/space image · Name + one-sentence concept · "הזמן שולחן" CTA
2. **Concept/Story** — 2–3 sentences. What makes this place different. No food clichés.
3. **Menu Preview** — 6–9 hero dishes in grid. Real dish names and descriptions. Prices: `[₪XX]` placeholder.
4. **Atmosphere Gallery** — Space photography. At least 4 images. Grid or masonry.
5. **Opening Hours + Location** — Embedded map, `<address>` with schema, Sunday–Saturday schedule
6. **Events / Specials** — Weekly specials, private events, catering. Only include if real.
7. **Booking CTA** — WhatsApp deeplink + inline form (name, date, party size, phone). Honeypot required.

**Aesthetic:** Dark, warm editorial. Frank Ruhl Libre display (very Israeli). Olive/amber/charcoal palette. Grain texture overlay. Generous food photography.

## SAAS / TECH / STARTUP

**Visitor mindset:** Skeptical, has seen 100 landing pages today. Wants: Does it do what I need? How fast? How much?

**Section order:**
1. **Hero** — One-sentence value proposition (not tagline) + product screenshot/demo video + CTA ("התחל חינם" / "Book a Demo")
2. **Social Proof Bar** — Company logos (grayscale) or user count. Real or absent.
3. **Problem → Solution** — Two-column: "בלעדינו" vs "אתנו". Concrete, specific differences.
4. **Features** — 3–6 features with screenshot/illustration per feature. Benefit-led headline.
5. **How It Works** — Numbered steps with real product UI screenshots
6. **Pricing** — 3 tiers (Starter / Growth / Enterprise) with feature comparison. Annual/monthly toggle.
7. **Testimonials** — Name, title, company, photo. No generic praise. Specific outcomes.
8. **FAQ** — Integration questions, pricing questions, security questions.
9. **Final CTA** — Repeat primary offer + urgency element if honest.

**Aesthetic:** Dark mode default. Geist Mono or Space Mono for code elements. Clean IBM Plex or custom geometric for UI. Phosphor green or electric blue accent on dark surface.

## LAW FIRM / LEGAL

**Visitor mindset:** In trouble or anticipating trouble. Needs authority, not friendliness.

**Section order:**
1. **Hero** — Specialization + track record signal + CTA ("ייעוץ ראשוני ללא עלות")
2. **Specializations** — Practice areas as cards. Brief, specific, outcome-oriented.
3. **Why This Firm** — Credentials, years, case outcomes (where legally permissible). No invented stats.
4. **Process** — "מה קורה אחרי שיצרת קשר" — 4-step intake process. Reduces anxiety.
5. **Attorney Profiles** — Real photo, real credentials, real bar number placeholder `[מס׳ רישיון]`
6. **FAQ** — Real questions the client has in their head. Answer directly.
7. **Contact** — Phone (prominent), WhatsApp, contact form. Office address with map.

**Aesthetic:** Authoritative dark navy or charcoal. Frank Ruhl Libre or Suez One for display. Gold/brass accent. No gradients. Zero animations beyond subtle reveals.

## CONSTRUCTION / CONTRACTOR / TRADES

**Visitor mindset:** Wants proof of work quality. Doesn't trust easily. Asks: "Are you licensed? Can I see your work?"

**Section order:**
1. **Hero** — Before/after or project image · Trade + location · CTA ("קבל הצעת מחיר")
2. **Services** — What you do, clearly listed. With icons. No vague bundling.
3. **Portfolio** — Real project photos with brief description. Placeholder: `[פרויקט [מיקום]]`.
4. **Why Us** — License number `[מס׳ רישיון]` · Years in business · Insurance coverage · Guarantees
5. **Process** — How a typical job goes start to finish. Answers: "Will my house be a mess for 3 months?"
6. **Testimonials** — Name and neighborhood only (for privacy). No fake text.
7. **CTA** — Quote form: name, phone, type of work, preferred date. Honeypot required.

**Aesthetic:** Retro craftsman. Condensed slab serif (Playfair condensed or custom). Rust/charcoal/sand palette. Blueprint grid texture. Badge-shaped CTA buttons.

## REAL ESTATE / PROPERTY

**Visitor mindset:** High-stakes decision. Wants data, not promises.

**Section order:**
1. **Hero** — Premium property image · Tagline · Search widget or "ראה נכסים" CTA
2. **Featured Listings** — 3–6 property cards: image, price, rooms, area, city
3. **Areas We Cover** — City/neighborhood grid with average pricing (if real data available)
4. **Why Us** — License `[מס׳ רישיון מתווך]` · Transaction count · Avg. days to sale
5. **Process** — Buy / sell / rent flows explained simply
6. **Team** — Real agents, real licensing. No invented personas.
7. **Contact / Valuation CTA** — "מה שווה הנכס שלך?" as lead magnet

**Aesthetic:** Clean and precise. Graphik or custom geometric sans. White/stone/black palette. Data-forward typography. Property images are the primary visual language.

## E-COMMERCE / FASHION / RETAIL

**Visitor mindset:** Browsing. Wants to discover, be inspired, be convinced.

**Section order:**
1. **Hero** — Campaign image · Season/offer · Shop CTA
2. **New Arrivals / Featured Products** — 4–8 product cards with real pricing
3. **Category Navigation** — Visual category grid
4. **Brand Story / Values** — 3–4 trust signals (production quality, sustainability, returns policy)
5. **Social Proof** — UGC photos or real reviews with star ratings
6. **Newsletter CTA** — Offer-based ("10% הנחה לנרשמים ראשונים")

**Aesthetic:** Fashion-editorial. Large photography. Minimal text. DM Serif Display or Cormorant for luxury; GT America Expanded for contemporary. Monochrome or muted palette with one bold accent.
